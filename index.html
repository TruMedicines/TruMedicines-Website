How TelePack works

Pre-packed doses

Local or online pharmacies supply pouches labeled by time and day. Compatible with PillPack, CVS and similar packets.

Dispense and verify

AI image recognition reads each packet, releases it to the right person, and logs adherence to the medical record.

Connect with care

One-click video calls with participating care providers, plus a talking medication assistant avatar that speaks 64 languages for scripted answers and check-ins.

Telehealth has a track record here. A review of 2,239 studies across 53 countries covering over 20,000 patients found benefits for medication adherence, chronic disease management, stress reduction and emotional support.

Sources: Journal of Cardiovascular Nursing; J Med Internet Res 2022;24(10):e40877.
