The Device

TelePacks Ai Managed Medication Dispenser

Award-winning and patented, TelePacks is an Ai smart dispenser — it speaks, listens, sees, and calls out for help. It reads pre-packaged medication or vitamins from Amazon PillPack, CVS, Walmart, Walgreens, Nurish, and Persona, but the product is the device itself: the voice, the screen, and the one-touch line to real care.

  • Recognition

    Ai image & speech recognition

    Reads each pre-packaged dose as it's dispensed and responds to spoken questions, so patients always know exactly what they're taking.

  • One-touch

    One-Touch Video calling

    A single button starts a Zoom-like video call — remote care and medication monitoring at the lowest possible cost per visit, for people with no technical skill required.

  • Avatar

    Conversational Avatar Assistant

    A multi-language, interactive Ai answers questions on demand. Staff can create personalized messages that offer encouragement and emotional support for patients in remote locations, low-resource settings, addiction treatment, or elder care.

  • Learning

    A network that learns treatment success

    Across text, audio, visual content, frequency, and duration, the Ai network learns over time the highest treatment success rate plan for medication and emotional therapy — all data encrypted and HIPAA compliant.

The workflow

From pharmacy to patient, in four steps

The same pipeline runs for every packet, every dose, every day.

01 · Order

Pre-packaged doses arrive

Pharmacy partners like PillPack, CVS, Walmart, or Walgreens ship pre-sorted medication or vitamin packets straight to the patient.

02 · Load

TelePacks reads the packet

Ai image recognition identifies the contents and timing printed on each packet before it's dispensed.

03 · Guide

Voice & Avatar guidance

The device speaks the dose aloud, answers questions, and delivers staff-authored emotional support messages.

04 · Monitor

Staff watch in real time

Every event flows to the Staff Web Portal, monitoring thousands of Telepack units and flagging patients who need outreach.
