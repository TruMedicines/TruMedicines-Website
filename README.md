# TruMedicines / TelePacks — Website
TelePacks Ai Managed Medication Dispenser
TelePacks Ai network will learn over time the highest treatment success rate plan for medication and emotional therapy. Medication management and emotional therapy have been neglected aspects of the healthcare system for many decades. 1/3 or 100 mil patients in USA do not have a regular doctor and are left to manage their own medications often with underdosing or overdosing is the common result, emotional therapy is often not part of the treatment plan. TelePacks is our award winning, patented Ai enabled medication management dispenser that uses Ai image and Ai speech recognition to identify pill-packet contents and respond to spoken requests (Alexa like). Our dispenser uses pre-packaged medications (or vitamins) from online pharmacies: Amazon PillPack, CVS, Walmart, Walgreens, Nurish, Persona. Our One-Touch Video call feature (zoom like) is one button Video conferencing which allows unskilled people to received remote care and medication monitoring at the lowest possible cost per visit.
Our Staff Web Portal dashboard provides real-time monitoring of 1,000’s of patient Telepack units. Our customized conversational Avatar Assistant is a multi-language interactive Ai asking and answering questions on demand. Personalized Avatar messages can be staff created to encourage and give emotional support to patients who are: in remote locations, low resource, addiction treatment, or elderly. Our Ai network will learn over time the highest treatment success rate plan for medication and emotional therapy: text, audio, visual content, frequency duration. All data is securely encrypted and compliant with HIPAA. Insurance & Medicare coverage CPT codes 99453, 99454, 99457, 99458, 99473, 99474, 99607, 99490. CMS pays for med adherence monitoring. CPT Billing: Doctors, Nurse, Social Worker, Occ. Therapist, Phy Therapist, Physician Assistant, Psychologist, Dietitian, Public Health/Welfare Agency, Lab, Diag Testing, Charitable Agencies. TruMedicines of Seattle, WA funded by HHS-FDA grant# IR43FD006302 to create Medication tracking mobile app to log self-reported health measures and track drug adherence. Patient drug adherence is securely logged to a central database of patient history. Medical staff can quickly and easily check daily status of multiple remote patients. Healthcare staff can respond to all needs of the patient in real-time monitoring.
